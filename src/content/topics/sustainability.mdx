## Planetary Stewardship and Carbon Sinks

Sustainability science examines how human resource consumption and land-use practices can be harmonized with Earth's natural biogeochemical boundaries to prevent catastrophic environmental degradation.

One of the most critical global sustainability challenges centers on **tropical peatland ecosystems**:
- Intact waterlogged peatlands accumulate dense organic matter over millennia, storing gigatonnes of carbon.
- Drainage canals and anthropogenic agricultural burning expose dried peat to atmospheric oxygen, fueling underground smoldering fires that release immense volumes of greenhouse gases and particulate matter (PM2.5).
- Restoring water tables through re-wetting and canal blocking offers a powerful ecological pathway for regional climate mitigation.
