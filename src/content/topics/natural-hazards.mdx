## Living with an Active Planet

The forces that make Earth habitable—tectonic mantle convection, volcanic degassing creating our atmosphere, and the hydrological water cycle—are the very same mechanisms that trigger natural hazards.

In regions like the Indonesian archipelago, situated at the triple convergence of the Indo-Australian, Eurasian (Sunda), and Pacific plates, disaster risk is a daily geological reality:

### Core Hazard Mechanisms
1. **Seismic Ruptures:** Elastic strain accumulation along locked faults culminating in sudden catastrophic rupture, seismic waves, and secondary liquefaction.
2. **Volcanic Hazards:** Pyroclastic density currents, explosive ash columns, and rainfall-induced lahars along active stratovolcanoes.
3. **Mass Wasting & Landslides:** Heavy monsoon rainfall saturating steep volcanic slopes, overcoming shear strength and causing devastating debris flows.
4. **Tsunamis:** Sudden displacement of water columns caused by undersea thrust earthquakes, submarine sediment collapse, or volcanic caldera explosions.
