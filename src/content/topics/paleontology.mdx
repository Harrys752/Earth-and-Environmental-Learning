## Chronicles of Ancient Life

Every organism that ever lived was part of an unbroken lineage stretching back over 3.7 billion years. Yet only a minuscule fraction of past organisms are ever preserved in stone.

Paleontologists combine field excavations, comparative anatomy, and geological stratigraphy to unlock these rare deep-time records:

### Key Paleontological Principles
1. **Fossilization Taphonomy:** How organic remains escape immediate decomposition through rapid burial in anoxic, mineral-rich sedimentary environments.
2. **Biostratigraphy & Index Fossils:** Using characteristic fossil species to correlate and date rock layers across continents.
3. **Hominid Evolution in the Pleistocene:** Investigating early human adaptation, toolmaking, and migration through rich fossil archives like Central Java's Sangiran Dome.
