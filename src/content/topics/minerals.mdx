## The Crystalline Architecture of Earth

Every rock, mountain range, and oceanic basin on Earth is constructed from **minerals**—naturally occurring, inorganic crystalline solids with ordered atomic lattices and definite chemical formulas.

Mineralogy is the cornerstone of Earth materials science. By examining properties such as crystal habit, Mohs hardness, cleavage planes, luster, and streak, geologists classify thousands of distinct mineral species and unlock the conditions under which they crystallized.

Furthermore, tropical weathering in equatorial regions like Indonesia drives the chemical enrichment of critical industrial minerals—such as **lateritic nickel**, which powers global energy transition technologies and modern stainless metallurgy.
