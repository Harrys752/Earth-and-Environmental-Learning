## The Hidden Waters of Earth

While rivers and oceans dominate our surface view, over **98% of Earth's liquid freshwater** is stored hidden underground in porous rock formations and sediment beds known as **aquifers**.

Hydrology investigates the movement, distribution, and chemical quality of water as it flows through the geological crust and atmosphere. In urbanized coastal deltas, groundwater systems interact directly with human populations:
- Pore-water pressure supports overlying soil layers and structural loads.
- Unregulated over-extraction causes unconfined and confined aquifers to lose pressure.
- Compressible clay beds suffer irreversible mechanical compaction, triggering catastrophic **land subsidence**.

Examine how hydrological physics shapes freshwater access, flood risks, and urban resilience in megacities like Jakarta.
