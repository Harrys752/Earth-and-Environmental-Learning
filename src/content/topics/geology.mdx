## Reading the Earth's Deep Time Archive

Human history spans a few thousand years; Earth history spans **4.54 billion years**. The physical record of this vast expanse of deep time is written directly into rock formations.

By studying the composition, fossil content, and geometrical orientation of rock layers, geologists reconstruct:
- Vanished oceans and ancient shorelines that covered continents.
- Mountain ranges that rose higher than the Himalayas and eroded away completely.
- Global mass extinctions and rapid biological recoveries.
