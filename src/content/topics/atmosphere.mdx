## The Planetary Atmosphere

Earth's atmosphere extends hundreds of kilometers into space, but the vast majority of its mass (about 75%) and almost all of its water vapor is compressed into the lowest layer: the **troposphere** (0 to 12 km altitude).

Within this active zone, solar energy drives the global hydrological cycle and circulation cells:
- **Hadley Cells:** Warm, moist equatorial air rises at the Intertropical Convergence Zone (ITCZ), creating low-pressure rain belts over tropical archipelagos like Indonesia, then descends as dry air in the subtropics.
- **Monsoonal Systems:** Seasonal shifting of heating between the Asian continent and Australian landmass produces the Northwest and Southeast Monsoons, governing agricultural seasons across Southeast Asia.
