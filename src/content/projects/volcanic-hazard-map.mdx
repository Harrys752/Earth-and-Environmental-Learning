## Project Overview

In this project, you will apply the geodynamic and petrological concepts learned in the flagship experience **Why Do Volcanoes Form?** to evaluate real-world human and infrastructural vulnerability around Mount Merapi, Central Java.

Merapi is surrounded by densely populated agricultural and urban areas, including the historic city of Yogyakarta just 28 kilometers to the south.

### Key Investigation Phases

1. **Hazard Characterization:** Distinguish between gravitational dome collapse events (generating block-and-ash flows) and explosive column collapse.
2. **Channel Topography & Lahar Dynamics:** Investigate how seasonal tropical rainfall mobilizes loose volcanic tephra into hyperconcentrated sediment-water flows (lahars) that destroy bridges and homes far beyond the immediate blast zone.
3. **Zoning & Mitigation Recommendations:** Formulate guidelines based on KRB III (highest danger zone prohibiting permanent settlement), KRB II, and KRB I.
