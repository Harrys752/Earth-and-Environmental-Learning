## What is Atmospheric Pressure?

Every square meter of Earth's surface at sea level bears the weight of roughly 10,000 kilograms of atmospheric air, exerting an average pressure of **101.3 kPa** (or 1013.25 millibars / hPa).

### Pressure Gradients and Wind

When the sun warms one geographic area more intensely than an adjacent area (such as land warming faster than water during the day), the air over the warmer surface expands, becomes buoyant, and rises. This leaves behind a localized **low-pressure zone**.

Nearby denser, cooler air flows horizontally toward this low-pressure center to replace the rising air. This horizontal movement of air in response to a pressure gradient is what we experience as **wind**.
