## Phase Change in the Atmosphere

Water vapor is a transparent gas composed of individual H₂O molecules moving freely through the air. As air cools, molecular motion slows down, and electrostatic attractions (hydrogen bonds) between water molecules begin to overcome their kinetic velocity.

### The Saturation Threshold & Dew Point

- **Saturation Vapor Pressure:** The maximum partial pressure of water vapor that can exist in equilibrium with liquid water at a given temperature.
- **Dew Point Temperature:** The temperature to which an air parcel must be cooled (at constant pressure) for water vapor to condense into liquid water.
- **Latent Heat Release:** When water vapor condenses into liquid droplets, it releases approximately 2,500 kJ/kg of latent heat into the surrounding air parcel. This warms the rising air, reducing its density and intensifying convective storm updrafts.
