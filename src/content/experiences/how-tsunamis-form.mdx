## Beat 1: The Energy of an Ocean in Motion

On the morning of **26 December 2004**, thousands of kilometers away in the deep waters of the Indian Ocean, ships sailed completely unaware that a catastrophic wave was passing directly beneath them.

The water rose less than a single meter as the wave passed under their hulls. Yet, within hours, that exact same wave struck the coast of **Aceh, Indonesia**, rising into a devastating wall of water **over 30 to 50 meters high** that swept kilometers inland.

How does energy released deep under the seafloor turn into an invisible deep-ocean wave that becomes a catastrophic wall of water at the coast?

---

## Beat 2: Tsunami Genesis — Megathrust Vertical Displacement

Ordinary ocean waves are generated by wind blowing across the water surface. A **tsunami**, by contrast, is generated by a massive physical movement of the **entire water column from seafloor to surface**.

The primary engine of major tsunamis is an **undersea megathrust earthquake** at a convergent subduction zone:

1. **Subduction Lock:** The oceanic Indo-Australian Plate grinds beneath the Sunda Plate. Friction locks the fault boundary, bending the overriding continental plate downward like a drawn bow over hundreds of years.
2. **Elastic Rupture:** When accumulated stress exceeds friction, the locked fault ruptures violently.
3. **Seafloor Rebound:** The overriding plate snaps upward by **10 to 20 meters** across thousands of square kilometers of seabed in seconds.
4. **Water Column Displacement:** This instantaneous vertical uplift pushes the entire overlying mass of ocean water upward, launching tsunami wave trains radiating outward across the ocean basin.

## Beat 3: The Physics of Shoaling — Fast in the Deep, Tall on the Shore

A tsunami in the open ocean behaves according to shallow-water wave physics governed by the water depth (`d`):

```
Wave Velocity: v = √(g × d)
```

where `g ≈ 9.8 m/s²` is gravitational acceleration.

### In the Deep Ocean (`d ≈ 4,000 m`):
- **Jet-Airliner Speed:** The wave travels at **~800 km/h** (200 m/s).
- **Enormous Wavelength:** The distance between wave crests is **100 to 200 kilometers**.
- **Imperceptible Height:** Because the immense energy is spread over such a vast wavelength, wave height is **less than 1 meter**.

### As the Wave Reaches Shallow Coastal Waters (`d < 50 m`):
- **Speed Drops:** Friction with the shallow seafloor slows the wave front to **30–50 km/h**.
- **Wavelength Compresses:** The back of the wave, still in deeper water, catches up to the slower front, compressing the wave from 100 km down to a few kilometers.
- **Shoaling Amplification:** Conservation of total wave energy forces the compressed water column upward. The low 1-meter swell surges into a towering, crushing wall of water (*shoaling effect*) with runup heights exceeding **20 to 51 meters** in Aceh.
